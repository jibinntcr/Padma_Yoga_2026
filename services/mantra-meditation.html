Mantra Meditation

The science of mantra is based on an understanding of sound vibrations which are primarily centred in various stations. Ultimate purpose of mantra is to go into supreme silence. Here we integrate mantra chanting and meditation with complete awareness and practising it to apply in day to day life for a balanced journey.This is a special practice of Himalayan yoga.
